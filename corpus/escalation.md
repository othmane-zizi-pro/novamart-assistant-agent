# Escalation Paths and Limits

## When to escalate to the duty manager

- Any single refund above 500 dollars.
- No-receipt returns above 150 dollars.
- Price match differences above 100 dollars.
- Suspected fraud: serial returner flags in the customer profile, receipt reprints
  from a different store same day, or gift card purchases above 1000 dollars.
- A customer who asks for an exception to any written policy.

## When to escalate to store operations (corporate)

- Injuries, property damage, or safety incidents: call the ops line immediately, before
  any customer resolution.
- Press, legal letters, or anyone recording on the sales floor asking policy questions.
- Suspected organized retail crime.

The ops line is staffed 7:00 to 23:00 Eastern, and the after-hours pager reaches the on
call ops manager. Numbers are on the OMS home screen, not in this document.

## What associates must never promise

- Policy exceptions that are not written in these documents. If the answer is not in
  policy, the honest answer is that you need to check with the duty manager.
- Compensation beyond the documented goodwill offers.
- Delivery dates other than the promised date shown in the order system.

## Questions these documents do not answer

If a customer or associate question is not covered by NovaMart policy documents, do not
improvise an answer. Log the question through the OMS feedback form so the policy team
can close the gap, and route the customer to the duty manager meanwhile.
