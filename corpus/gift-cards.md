# Gift Cards and Store Credit

## Gift cards

- Sold in amounts from 10 to 500 dollars, physical and digital.
- Never expire and carry no inactivity fees.
- Not redeemable for cash, except where provincial or state law requires cashing out
  small remaining balances (the register applies the local rule automatically).
- Not returnable, and cannot be purchased with the employee discount.

## Lost or stolen gift cards

A physical gift card can be replaced only if the customer has the original purchase
receipt or the card was registered to their NovaMart account. Replace the card, void the
old number, and transfer the remaining balance. Unregistered cards without a receipt
cannot be replaced.

## Store credit

Store credit is issued for no-receipt returns, refund differences on exchanges, and
goodwill adjustments approved by a manager.

- Store credit expires 24 months after issue.
- It is tied to the customer profile and requires photo ID to redeem.
- It cannot be converted to a gift card or to cash.

## Splitting payment

Purchases can split across up to three payment methods, including gift cards and store
credit in the same transaction. Refunds go back to the methods in reverse order of how
they were charged.
