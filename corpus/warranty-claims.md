# Warranty Claims

## When warranty applies

A warranty claim is the right flow when an item is defective and its return window has
passed (15 days for electronics, 30 days otherwise). Inside the return window, a
defective item is a straight return or even exchange instead.

## Manufacturer warranty

Most electronics carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty. NovaMart handles the first step
in store so the customer does not chase the manufacturer:

- Items with a current selling price of 200 dollars or less: replace on the spot from
  shelf stock and ship the defective unit back through the store's RMA bin.
- Items above 200 dollars: create a service ticket in the OMS; the unit goes to the
  regional service center, with a 7 to 10 business day turnaround. Offer a loaner phone
  or laptop from the service shelf when available.

## NovaCare extended protection

NovaCare extends coverage to 3 years total and adds accidental damage (drops, spills)
for eligible electronics. NovaCare claims skip the manufacturer step entirely: replace
or repair per the same 200-dollar threshold, regardless of what caused the damage, up to
two accidental claims per plan.

## Proof of purchase

Warranty and NovaCare claims require the purchase to be findable: receipt, order number,
or the customer's account history. Look the order up by order number or customer email in
the order system before promising anything. If the purchase cannot be found, the claim
goes directly to the manufacturer and the store cannot intervene.
