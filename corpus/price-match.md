# Price Match Guarantee

## What we match

NovaMart matches the current advertised price of identical, in-stock items from:

- Major national online retailers (their list is maintained in the OMS help page).
- Local competitors with a physical store within 40 kilometers.
- novamart.com itself, when the online price is lower than the shelf price.

The item must be identical: same model number, size, and color, sold new.

## When we match

- At the time of purchase.
- Within 7 days after purchase: refund the difference to the original payment method.

## What we do not match

- Marketplace and third-party sellers, on any site.
- Doorbusters, flash sales, limited-quantity offers, and clearance prices.
- Refurbished, open-box, or used items.
- Prices requiring a membership, coupon, or financing offer.
- Free-with-purchase bundles.

## Approval limits

Associates may approve price matches up to a difference of 100 dollars. Above 100
dollars, the duty manager verifies the competitor price and approves. Employee discount
does not stack on top of a matched price; the customer or employee picks one.
