# Returns and Refunds Policy

## Standard return window

Most items may be returned within 30 days of purchase for a full refund to the original
payment method. The item must be unused, in its original packaging, and accompanied by a
receipt or order number.

## Electronics

Electronics (computers, tablets, phones, TVs, audio, gaming hardware) have a shorter
return window: 15 days from purchase.

- Unopened electronics: full refund within 15 days.
- Opened electronics: refundable within 15 days with a 15 percent restocking fee,
  unless the item is defective. Defective items are always exchanged or refunded in
  full within the 15-day window; after 15 days, defective electronics go through the
  warranty claim process instead of a return.

## Clothing and apparel

Clothing may be returned within 30 days if unworn, unwashed, and with original tags
attached. Swimwear and undergarments are final sale.

## Final sale items

The following are not returnable: clearance items marked Final Sale, gift cards, opened
software and video games, personalized or altered items, and perishables.

## Returns without a receipt

If the customer has no receipt and the purchase cannot be found in the order system,
offer store credit at the item's lowest selling price in the last 90 days. A
government-issued photo ID is required and is recorded against the return. Associates
may not process a no-receipt return above 150 dollars; call the duty manager.

## Refund timing

- Original payment card: 5 to 7 business days after the return is accepted.
- Store credit: issued immediately at the register.
- Cash purchases above 200 dollars are refunded by corporate check within 10 business
  days, not from the register drawer.
