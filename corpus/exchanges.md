# Exchanges Policy

## Exchange windows

Exchanges follow the same windows as returns: 30 days for most items, 15 days for
electronics. An exchange does not restart the return window; the original purchase date
still applies.

## Even exchanges

Size and color swaps of the same product are free and unlimited within the window, with
tags attached for apparel. No restocking fee applies to even exchanges, including opened
electronics exchanged for the identical model because of a defect.

## Exchanges with a price difference

If the replacement item costs more, the customer pays the difference at today's price.
If it costs less, refund the difference to the original payment method, or to store
credit for no-receipt exchanges.

## Defective items outside the window

A defective item past its return window is not an exchange: it is a warranty claim. See
the Warranty Claims policy. Do not process warranty situations through the exchange flow;
the registers report exchanges as sales metrics and it corrupts store numbers.

## Cross-store exchanges

Any NovaMart store can accept an exchange for an item bought at another NovaMart store,
as long as the purchase is findable in the order system. Marketplace items sold by
third-party sellers on novamart.com cannot be exchanged in store; direct the customer to
the seller through their online account.
