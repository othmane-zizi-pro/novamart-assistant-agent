# Employee Discount Program

## Discount rates

- 10 percent off storewide.
- 20 percent off NovaMart private-label brands (NovaHome, NovaBasics, NovaTech
  accessories).

The discount applies in store and on novamart.com when the employee account is linked to
the store badge number.

## Eligibility

All active employees from their first day, plus one linked household member. The linked
member gets the same rates. Retired employees with 10 or more years of service keep the
10 percent rate.

## What the discount does not apply to

- Gift cards and prepaid cards.
- Items already price matched (pick one: the match or the discount).
- Clearance items marked Final Sale.
- Marketplace items from third-party sellers.
- Postage, fees, and services.

## Stacking rules

The employee discount stacks with public promotions and coupons unless the promotion
terms say otherwise, but never stacks with a price match. On private-label items the 20
percent rate replaces, not adds to, the 10 percent rate.

## Misuse

Buying for resale, or for people outside the linked household, is misuse. First
incident: written warning and discount suspension for 90 days. Second incident:
termination. Purchases made with the discount are audited monthly.
